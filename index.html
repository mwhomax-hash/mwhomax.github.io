MyFitBitAirAnalyz — Privacy Policy

MyFitBitAirAnalyz is a personal, non-commercial application used solely by its developer (mwhomax@gmail.com) to analyze his own Fitbit/Google Health data.

Data accessed via Google APIs is stored only locally on the developer's own computer. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
